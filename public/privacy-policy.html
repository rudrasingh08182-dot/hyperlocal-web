Privacy Policy

Zaplo — Hyperlocal Commerce App  •  Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Zaplo ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information from Buyers, Sellers, and Delivery Partners who use our platform. By using Zaplo, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, phone number, username, and password (stored securely as a hashed value, never in plain text).
  • Location data: with your permission, we collect precise GPS location — for Buyers, to calculate delivery charges and show your delivery address; for Sellers, to set your shop location; for Delivery Partners, to show nearby orders and share live tracking with the Buyer during delivery.
  • Order and payment information: order history, delivery address, and payment method. Online payments are processed securely by Razorpay; Zaplo does not store your card, UPI, or bank credentials.
  • Seller bank details: for Sellers, account holder name, bank name, account number, IFSC code, and UPI ID, used solely to process payouts via Razorpay.
  • Photos: product images uploaded by Sellers, stored via Cloudinary.
  • Reviews and ratings: any reviews or ratings you submit for products or shops.
  • Device and usage data: basic technical information (such as device type and app interactions) used to maintain and improve the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity when you log in.
  • To process and deliver your orders, including calculating delivery charges based on distance.
  • To process payments and, for Sellers, to send payouts to your saved bank account.
  • To show live order tracking between Delivery Partners and Buyers.
  • To send you order-related notifications.
  • To maintain platform safety, including Seller and Delivery Partner approval and account moderation.

4. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only as needed to operate the platform:

  • Buyers and Sellers/Delivery Partners: to fulfill an order, we share the Buyer's delivery address and phone number with the assigned Seller and Delivery Partner, and the Seller's shop details with the Buyer.
  • Razorpay: for processing payments and Seller payouts.
  • Cloudinary: for hosting product images.
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL): our database provider, which stores your account and order data securely.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of our users.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted password storage (bcrypt), JWT-based authentication, and HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your device and our servers.

6. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to grant location permission; some features (like delivery charge calculation and live tracking) require it to function.
  • You can update your account details from within the app.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

Zaplo is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
